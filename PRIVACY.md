# My Teams privacy policy

_Last updated: 3 November 2026_

My Teams is an unofficial fan app for following sports teams. It is not affiliated with any team, league or data provider.

## What the app stores

- **Your favourite teams and score alert choices.** These are saved only on your phone. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No collection of personal information, location, contacts or device identifiers.
- No ads, analytics or tracking.
- Nothing is sold or shared.

## Services the app contacts

To show stats, news and highlights, the app requests public data directly from:

- **TheSportsDB** (thesportsdb.com) for teams, results, fixtures and tables
- **Google News** (news.google.com) for headlines
- **YouTube** (youtube.com), only when you tap a highlight

These requests contain only the team you are looking at. Like any website, these services can see your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply.

## Notifications

Score alerts are off for every team until you switch them on for a specific team. Android will ask your permission first. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in Android settings.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
